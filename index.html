Welcome

Looking for a trusted, high-quality tattoo studio in Leeds?

At Black Bright Tattoo, we offer expert tattooing in a friendly, professional and inclusive environment. With over 20 years of combined experience, our talented artists specialise in a wide range of styles.

We’re proud to be known for our clean, comfortable and fully licensed studio, where your health and safety always come first. Our team is fully qualified, insured and dedicated to delivering tattoos you’ll love for life.
